## What it is

Royal Valley Luxury Restrooms: a locally owned luxury restroom-trailer rental business based in Walla Walla, WA. One climate-controlled trailer with three private stations, rented for special events in Walla Walla and nearby.

## Users and job

Mixed event bookers, roughly equal weight: couples and wedding planners, corporate event organizers, outdoor festival runners, private-party hosts. Their job: secure a restroom their guests will be comfortable using, without logistics headaches, and book it by phone or form.

## Confirmed product facts

- 3 stations, climate controlled (air conditioning), rough footprint 12′ × 8′.
- Steps up to each door with supportive handrails; easy access for any age.
- Upscale decor, premium toiletries, modern fixtures, vanity with sink and mirror, wood-look floor, urinal and toilet in station interior (from photos).
- "We roll out the green carpet": artificial-turf mat at the steps.
- Night lighting so events can keep going after dark.
- Venue requirements: a power outlet and a hose (water) outlet only. RVL brings all hoses and cables.
- Serves weddings, corporate events, outdoor festivals, and more.
- Started as a start-up project to bring luxury to every occasion.
- Values: Excellence, Innovation, Client Satisfaction.
