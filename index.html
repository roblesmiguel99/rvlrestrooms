Front desk

Common questions. Anything else is a phone call away: (509) 212-6575.

What should I have at my venue?

Only a power outlet and a hose outlet. See what your venue needs.

Do I need any hoses or cables?

No. We take care of everything so the rental runs smoothly and efficiently.

How much space does the trailer need?

The trailer is roughly 12′ × 8′, plus room for the steps at each of the three doors.

What kinds of events do you serve?

Weddings, corporate events, outdoor festivals, private parties, and more, in Walla Walla and nearby locations.

Can guests of any age use it comfortably?

Yes. Each door has steps with supportive handrails for easy access.
